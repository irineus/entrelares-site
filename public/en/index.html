From one parent to another: why I built Entrelares

This app wasn't born in a business meeting. It was born out of a real need, inside my own family.

I was married for 20 years, and from that marriage came the most precious thing in my life: my son. When his mother and I understood that the marriage would not go on, we chose to divorce with everyone's good in mind — his above all.

Suddenly everything was new: the house, the routine, the habits. But one thing didn't change — the wish to be present in my son's life. Amid the natural friction of a recent divorce, what we needed most was exactly what seemed hardest: giving stability and predictability to a small child, around his mother's demanding work schedule.

Both of us needed to know clearly whose day each day was. I tried a spreadsheet — it helped at first, but it soon got complicated and was never at hand when it mattered. I tried a regular calendar — too much work to keep up. So I built something of my own, simple, and it grew until it became a real partner in the time I spend with my son.

Talking with other parents who had been through a divorce, I heard the same gap. That's why I decided to open this tool up to more families — to take that friction out of the way at such a delicate moment, one that is overcome with care, love and, above all, respect between everyone involved.

Install it on your phone in 30 seconds

Entrelares is a web app (PWA): it installs straight from the browser, with no app store, and opens like any other app — with an icon on your home screen.

🤖 Android (Chrome)

  1. Go to app.entrelares.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when the prompt appears — or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app.
  3. Done: the icon shows up on your home screen, like any other app.

🍎 iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Go to app.entrelares.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen and confirm.

Frequently asked questions

From the concept to everyday use — what parents ask most before getting started.

How does child custody work after a divorce?
Every family lands on its own arrangement — formal joint custody, sole custody with visitation, or an agreement that was never taken to court. In all of them the practical challenge is the same: knowing who has the child on each day, how holidays and vacations work, and what to do when one of you needs to swap a day. That everyday agreement is what the app organizes — whatever shape the custody takes.
What is a parenting calendar?
It's the agreement that defines whose day each day is — alternating weeks, fixed days, rotating weekends, holidays. When it lives only in each parent's head, or scattered across a chat, it becomes a source of friction. Entrelares puts that calendar in one place, identical for both parents, with every day swap approved by the other parent and recorded with date and time — less arguing about what was agreed, and more predictability for the children.
Is the app available in English?
Yes. The whole app interface is available in English and Portuguese, and you can switch language at any time in your profile. What is never translated is what you write yourself — names, notes and messages stay exactly as you typed them. Prices are charged in Brazilian reais (see Pricing).
Does the other parent need an account too?
The app works best with both of you: you create the family and send an invitation by e-mail. It's with two accounts that day swaps gain mutual approval — the heart of the tool. You can start on your own and invite the other parent whenever you want.
Can the history be used as evidence in court?
The app permanently records every swap, approval and change, with date and time, in a history that cannot be edited or deleted. That record can be useful to document how the parenting agreement was followed — but whether any document counts as evidence is always the judge's decision, and the rules differ from country to country. When in doubt, talk to your lawyer.
Are my data and my children's data safe?
Yes. Each family sees only its own data — that separation is enforced in the database, not just on screen. We show no ads and sell no data, and we follow the LGPD, Brazil's data protection law. See our Privacy Policy (published in Portuguese).
How much does the app cost?
The essentials are free: the calendar for both parents, swaps approved by both, notifications and the history. Premium costs R$ 5,49/month (or R$ 54,90/year, with 2 months free) per family — a promotional launch price, a single amount that covers every caregiver — and unlocks advanced features such as adding grandparents, a nanny and other caregivers beyond the couple, the history report as a PDF and planning up to 24 months ahead. Payment is by Pix (a QR code in your banking app, no card details) or credit card, with a full refund within 7 days (article 49 of the Brazilian Consumer Code); cancel whenever you like without losing any data.
Does it work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. The app runs on any phone with a browser — Android or iPhone — and can be installed on the home screen in seconds, with no app store involved. See the installation steps.
What if the other parent doesn't approve a swap?
The swap simply doesn't happen: the day stays exactly as the calendar has it, and the refusal is recorded in the history. There's one rule — no day changes without both parents agreeing. That's what keeps the calendar trustworthy for both of them.
